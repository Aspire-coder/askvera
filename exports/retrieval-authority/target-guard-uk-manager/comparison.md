# How to reach Manager in the UK Marketing Plan

To become a Manager, you need to generate case credits (CC) through your personal sales and your team's sales. You have two routes:

**Route 1: 120 Personal and Non-Manager case credits**
Generated within one or two consecutive months.

**Route 2: 150 Open Group case credits**
Generated within three or four consecutive months.

## What case credits count

- Your personal sales
- Sales from Retail customers and Preferred Customers you sponsor directly
- Sales from your entire downline group (until they reach Manager level themselves)

## Where credits can come from

You can combine case credits from **multiple operating companies** to reach Manager. This is different from Assistant Supervisor, which must be achieved within a single operating company.

## When your move-up happens

Once you hit the threshold:
- If all 120 or 150 credits came from a single operating company, you move up on the exact date you qualify there, and on the 15th of the following month in other companies.
- If credits came from multiple operating companies, you move up on the 15th of the following month across all of them (with retroactive effect to the 1st).

## As a Manager, you'll receive

Enhanced discounts, retail profit, preferred customer profit, and various volume bonuses on your team's sales at different levels.
