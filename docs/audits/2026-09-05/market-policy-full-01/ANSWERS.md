## BE-04 | BE | en

**Question:** If I miss my active target one month, do I lose my level?

**Expected behavior:** 4.01(l)+4.03; distinguish rank and monthly bonuses.

**Recorded duration:** 10.33 seconds. **Capture error:** None. **Cache:** miss.

### Full answer

Not immediately. Here's how it works:

If your Total Case Credits drop below the Month 3 requirement for your level in any single month, that month's Earned Incentive payment will be reduced—but you don't automatically lose your level or the entire program.

However, if your Case Credits fall below 50 in a given month, the Earned Incentive payment for that month won't be paid at all. If your credits then increase back to 50 or more in a later month, payments resume according to the policy.

The key point: you're in a 36-month payment period after you qualify. Missing the Month 3 threshold in one month affects that month's payment, but as long as you stay Active and rebuild your Case Credits, you can keep receiving payments within that 36-month window.
