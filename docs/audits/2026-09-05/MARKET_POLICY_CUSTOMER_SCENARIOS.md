## Findings that should change the evaluation

1. **Return rules are not universal.** Reviewed customer windows are 30 days from purchase in BE/NL/CA/US, 60 in UK, 90 in Sweden. Germany has a specific satisfaction procedure with condition and evidence requirements. Italy has separate withdrawal and satisfaction procedures with different clock-start events.
2. **Sponsor changes depend on role and edition.** US 4.02 and 14 are intentionally deleted. Italy distinguishes Promoter, Cliente Premium and FBO. A generic six-month answer is unsafe.
3. **Activity is not rank retention or guaranteed earnings.** US 4.03(b) specifies 2 Active CC domestically; several other markets require 4 including at least 1 Personal CC. Do not turn Personal CC into an unsupported requirement to personally spend a fixed cash amount.
4. **Order minimum is not enrollment price or CC conversion.** Germany specifies product value exceeding EUR 100, excluding promotional material. UK has first-order and role-specific rules. Neither establishes the cash cost of 2CC.
5. **Source inconsistencies must not be hidden.** US deleted sponsor-change sections coexist with international cross-references; Italy has overlapping role and return wording. These are content-owner questions, not automatically retrieval defects.

## Separate ambiguity queue before deterministic gold answers

- US: deleted 4.02/14 versus section 15 references; domestic 2CC versus retained international 4CC language; 21.05(a,d) omissions versus (b) terminating-FBO wording. Content owner should approve applicability, not engineers invent a reconciliation.
- Italy: 4.02 and 14.01 use different Promoter timing descriptions; 13.01(a) no personal order versus (e) Starter Kit; 21.03 delivery-based withdrawal versus 21.06 purchase-based satisfaction. Preserve both governing routes and ask for missing context.
- Germany: minimum-order verb means exceeding EUR 100, not automatically at least EUR 100; confirm exact-boundary operation before a checkout guarantee. 4.03(c) role-list repetition and 21 cross-reference numbering need editorial review.
- Canada: inspect definitions before inferring the full permitted makeup of Active CC from 4.03(b) alone.
- Global/local overlaps: the directory's no-first-PC-order statement is not an FBO qualification rule. Resolve source version and role before using it alongside local first-order minimums. No universal precedence rule was established by this review.

## How to evaluate, without tuning to these exact strings

1. Freeze the supplied file hashes, market, source language, role, effective version, pages and sections for each case. Separate editorial ambiguities from supported-answer cases.
2. Run Current cache-bypassed through the supported test mechanism. Record the exact question, full answer, citations, retrieved top candidates, selected evidence, rejection reason and timing. Do not silently clear shared caches.
3. Score retrieval coverage, selected evidence, final correctness/completeness, numeric/time-anchor accuracy, conversational relevance and boundary behavior separately. Ambiguity cases pass by calibrated clarification, not invented specificity.
4. For any candidate, compare the same inputs and frozen environment, keep both full answers, repeat variable model stages, and report per-market counts rather than one vague improvement percentage.
5. Keep local-policy isolation tests and globally accessible sponsoring questions as separate boundary cases. Add natural follow-ups, typos, exact-boundary amounts/dates and mixed allowed/disallowed intent without weakening safety thresholds.
6. Re-ground the older 40-question draft before reuse. These 40 cases are a new targeted extension, not proof every old case is repaired or that the bot now passes.
