Does keeping my sales level mean I'm automatically active every month?

### Repeat 1

**Current** | 12.709389999999999s | answer returned

No, keeping your sales level does not automatically mean you're active every month. These are separate requirements.

**Sales Level vs. Activity Status**

Once you earn a sales level (Assistant Supervisor, Supervisor, Assistant Manager, or Manager), you keep that level—there's no re-qualifying for it unless your Forever Business is terminated or you're re-sponsored. However, your sales level and your active status are different things.

**What Active Status Requires Each Month**

To be considered Active in your Home Operating Company during a calendar month, you must earn a total of 4 Active Case Credits in that month. If you're looking to qualify as Active in a Foreign Operating Company, you either need to have qualified as Active in your Home Operating Company the previous month, or earn 4 Active Case Credits in that Foreign Operating Company during that month.

**Why This Matters**

If you don't achieve Active status in a given month, you won't receive certain bonuses that month—even though you keep your sales level. Any bonuses you would have earned get paid upline to Active FBOs instead. You can re-qualify as Active the following month with no retroactive payment.

So your sales level is permanent (once earned), but your active status must be earned fresh each month to receive the bonuses tied to it.
