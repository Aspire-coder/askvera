No, keeping your sales level does not mean you're automatically active every month.

Here's the key distinction: **Sales Level and Activity Status are separate.**

Once you've earned a Sales Level (Assistant Supervisor, Supervisor, Assistant Manager, or Manager), you retain that level—there's no re-qualifying for it unless your Forever Business is terminated or you're re-Sponsored. That's your permanent rank.

However, **Activity Status must be earned each month.** To be considered Active in your Home Operating Company during a calendar month, you must have a total of **4 Active Case Credits** in that month. If you don't achieve 4 Active Case Credits, you won't be Active that month—even if you hold a Sales Level.

This matters because to qualify for Volume Bonuses, Leadership Bonuses, and Incentives, you must be Active during the month in which those bonuses are accrued, in addition to meeting all other Marketing Plan requirements. If you're not Active in a given month, any bonuses you've accrued that month go upline to Active FBOs instead.

The good news: if you forfeit a bonus by not being Active one month, you can re-qualify as Active the following month with no retroactive application.
