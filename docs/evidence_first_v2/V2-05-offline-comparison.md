# V2-05 offline comparison contract

V2-05 is a closed-world, offline structural comparison harness. It measures a saved baseline replay against an injected deterministic candidate replay. It is not an end-to-end old/new pipeline measurement, real-source evaluation, held-out quality claim, live-model test, or aggregate quality score.

The executable surface is deliberately limited to `admit_owned`, `run_owned`, `validate_report`, and `summarize`. The application reloads reviewed records and a versioned pre-exposure ledger internally before callbacks. Generic caller-created inventories, reviewer registries, manifests, and reports are not executable. A manifest is rejected before callbacks if authority, source/span/version, ledger, ordered record membership, implementation, evaluator, or comparison identity is stale or substituted.

`comparison_exposure.py` is the versioned application-owned pre-exposure artifact. Its identity is bound to every manifest. It conservatively records reviewed development family and source-family/version lineage, so reset, relabel, span/version change, or unavailable promotion cannot create a held-out executable case in this fixed fixture.

The fixture is synthetic development evidence, hand-authored before replay evaluation. Its eight scenarios are `manager`, `p001_identity`, `public_contact`, `role_figure`, `policy_sponsoring`, `multilingual_followup`, `refusal`, and `harmful_claim`. Each has source-bound satisfying and targeted violating constraints: manager conditions, entity identity, Kenya public phone, role/unit/timing, US-policy versus Kenya sponsoring, an English-to-Spanish explicit destination change, authorized versus false refusal, and affirmative harmful claim versus a safe negative clause. Real-source development and reserved-held-out are explicit unavailable records; they run no adapters or evaluator.

Each observation binds the case, reviewer record, replay content digest, side, evaluator version, and exactly seven named Boolean metrics. `True` always means the reviewed constraint passes, including no false refusal, no unsupported claim, and no scope violation. Transitions are explicit per metric: false-to-true improvement, true-to-false regression, unchanged-pass, unchanged-fail, or not-comparable when a pair is missing. No score, weights, or offsetting safety regression exists. Result validation is centralized for generated and retained reports: it reloads owned replay and observation bindings, recomputes transitions, and enforces exact reason-specific payload shapes (no payload for unavailable evidence/baseline failure, baseline only for candidate failure, two replays/no observations for evaluator fault, and paired payload only for availability).

`POSITIVE_CANDIDATES`, `NEGATIVE_CANDIDATES`, and `fixture_evaluator` are reviewer-authored synthetic controls. Each of the eight rows has a positive candidate replay and one targeted negative replay that deterministically fails its named metric. They are explicit harness controls, not saved pipeline output.

Reports bind manifest/run identities, exact ordered unique result identities, transitions, and all four provenance-by-split axes. Each axis reports total, available, unavailable, and per-metric paired denominators; a zero comparable denominator is `N/A` (`None`), not a success rate. Synthetic and real-source, and development and reserved-held-out, remain separate.

Limit: hashes prove consistency with application-owned synthetic fixture data, not reviewer authorship or hostile-Python isolation. Trusted injected local adapters/evaluators remain test infrastructure. Missing real-source, held-out, old-pipeline, retrieval, translation, and live-provider evidence stays visibly unavailable.
